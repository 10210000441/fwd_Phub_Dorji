What we do

Every step of the application, handled properly.

Six services that cover the whole journey. Take all of them, or only the one you're stuck on.

Course & university matching

We shortlist 6–8 programmes against your grades, budget and career plan — with entry odds stated honestly for each.

Applications & SOP editing

Line-by-line editing of your statement of purpose, CV and recommendation briefs, then full submission handling.

Scholarships & funding

We track merit awards, need-based grants and assistantships, and build your funding case before deadlines close.

Visa & documentation

Financial evidence, file assembly and mock interviews with a counsellor who knows what each embassy asks.

IELTS & test preparation

Small-batch IELTS, PTE and SAT classes with weekly mock tests and a band-score guarantee on the full course.

Pre-departure & housing

Accommodation search, airport pickup, banking, SIM cards and a briefing on life in your host city before you fly.

Where students go

Six destinations we know inside out.

Filter by what matters most to you — cost, work rights or time to a decision.

Australia

Avg. tuition AUD 32k/yr · 2–4 yr work visa

Strong in engineering, nursing and IT. February and July intakes, with quick conditional offers.

Canada

Avg. tuition CAD 26k/yr · up to 3 yr PGWP

Co-op programmes that pay while you study, and a clear route from graduation to residency.

Germany

Public tuition near zero · 18-month job seeker visa

Tuition-free public universities. You'll need blocked-account funds and, for some courses, German.

United Kingdom

Avg. tuition £18k/yr · 2 yr graduate route

One-year master's degrees — the fastest way to a UK qualification if your budget is tight on time.

Japan

Avg. tuition ¥820k/yr · MEXT scholarships

Generous government scholarships and a growing number of English-taught engineering degrees.

Singapore

Avg. tuition SGD 30k/yr · regional hub

Close to home, globally ranked, and the strongest option for finance and computing careers in Asia.

How it works

Five steps, roughly nine months.

You'll always know which step you're on and what's due next.

  1. 01

    Free profile assessment

    A 45-minute session on your grades, budget and goals. You leave with a realistic destination list — no obligation.

  2. 02

    Shortlist & test plan

    We lock 6–8 universities into safe, match and reach tiers, and set your IELTS or SAT dates around their deadlines.

  3. 03

    Applications submitted

    Documents edited, forms filed, fees tracked. We chase every university that goes quiet on your file.

  4. 04

    Offers, funding & visa

    We compare your offers side by side, apply for scholarships, and prepare the full visa file and mock interview.

  5. 05

    Departure & first month

    Housing confirmed, flights booked, and a check-in call after you arrive to make sure you've settled in.
